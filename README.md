# The 3 AM Excuse

A one-line excuse in the voice of a hardware wallet that refuses to sign a transaction at 3 AM. Instead of the usual battery, firmware or cold-storage jokes, the wallet blames its own secure element — a co-worker who clocked out at midnight and holds grudges — and frames the 3 AM transaction as the impulse buy it is. The hour is the joke: the refusal only makes sense at 3 AM, when the person asking is up late and the device's most guarded part is off the clock.

> My secure element clocked out at midnight and it holds grudges. I do not wake it up for a 3 AM impulse buy.

The entry is 107 characters. It fits the brief because it is on subject (a hardware wallet refusing to sign at 3 AM), finds a fresh angle (the secure element as a grudge-holding colleague who clocked out), speaks in the device's own voice ("I do not wake it up"), turns the 3 AM hour itself into the joke, and lands in one tight line with no explanation of its own punchline.
